# ShankhNaad changes · 18 Nov 2026

## Two new pages on the left rail: Calls and Files (like Microsoft Teams)
- **Calls** (phone icon, under Chat): every call in your chats, newest first, grouped by day (Today, Yesterday, …).
  - Each row: the person (with photo and green/grey dot) or group, ↑ outgoing / ↓ incoming, and the result: talk time (e.g. 4m 12s), Missed (in red), No answer, Declined, Cancelled, or **In progress · Join**.
  - Tabs: **All · Missed · Incoming · Outgoing**, plus search by name.
  - Buttons on each row: **Call** again, **Chat**.
  - Top cards: calls in the last 30 days, missed, total talk time, people called.
  - A red count on the Calls icon after a missed call, cleared when you open Calls.
- **Files** (folder icon, under Meetings): every file shared with you or by you, and the chat it's in.
  - Each row: a coloured file-type tile (PDF, Word, Excel, image, …), name, size, who shared it, **with whom / in which group**, time.
  - Tabs: **All · Shared by you · Shared with you**; type chips (PDF, Image, Spreadsheet, …); search by file, person or group.
  - Buttons: **Download**, **Chat** (opens the chat it was shared in).
  - Top cards: files, shared by you, shared with you, total size.
- Both pages update by themselves when a call ends or a file is shared.
- **Run `database/23_CallsAndFiles.sql`** (or `99_ApplyAllUpdates.sql`; needs 22 first). Until then the pages show a reminder.
- Files: `23_CallsAndFiles.sql`, `99_ApplyAllUpdates.sql`, `ChatDtos.cs`, `CallLogService.cs`, `HistoryController.cs` (new), `ViewModels.cs`, `Views/History/Calls.cshtml` + `Files.cshtml` (new), `_Layout.cshtml`, `wwwroot/js/history.js` (new), `site.js`, `shankhnaad.css`.

## Calling again after a decline didn't ring
- **Cause**: the live connection gave up after about a minute without network (PC asleep, Edge "sleeping tab", Wi-Fi blip) and never came back, so that page got no calls or messages until it was refreshed. It showed as offline (grey dot / "0 online").
- **Fixes**
  - The connection now keeps reconnecting for ever, and again the moment the tab is opened or the network returns.
  - A page that wakes up asks "is anyone calling me?" and shows the ringing card with the ring for any call that is still ringing (up to 40 s old).
  - A call already declined, answered or missed on a screen never rings there a second time.
  - The caller is told inside the call when the other person isn't online: "sandipan isn't online right now, so it won't ring. They'll see a missed call."
- SQL: `23_CallsAndFiles.sql` now also has `sahyog_CallLog_Pending` (run 23 or 99 again; safe).
- Files: `23_CallsAndFiles.sql`, `99_ApplyAllUpdates.sql`, `CallLogService.cs`, `ChatHub.cs`, `MeetingsController.cs`, `ViewModels.cs`, `Room.cshtml`, `site.js`, `room.js`.

## Delete on the Calls and Files pages
- Each row has a 🗑 button; **Select** lets you tick several (or click the rows) and **Delete selected (n)**; **Delete all** clears the whole list. Every delete asks first.
- **Calls**: removed from *your* call history only; the other person keeps theirs. The call card in the chat stays.
- **Files**:
  - someone else's file: **Remove from my list** (it stays in the chat).
  - a file you shared: choose **Delete for everyone** (gone from the chat and from storage, frees space) or **Remove from my list**.
  - Select / Delete all only clean up your list; nothing is deleted from chats.
- **Run `database/24_HistoryDelete.sql`** (or `99_ApplyAllUpdates.sql`). If you ever re-run 23 on its own, run 24 after it.
- Files: `24_HistoryDelete.sql` (new), `99_ApplyAllUpdates.sql`, `CallLogService.cs`, `HistoryController.cs`, `Views/History/Calls.cshtml`, `Files.cshtml`, `history.js`, `shankhnaad.css`.

## Meetings page: delete, and a cleaner calendar
- **Delete a meeting**: hover a meeting in the Day, Week or Month view and press the red 🗑 (only on meetings you set up, or any if you're a workspace admin). Or click a meeting and press **Delete** in the header (was "Cancel"). It asks first and removes it from everyone's calendar; you stay on the same day/week/month.
- **Delete ended**: one button deletes every ended meeting you set up in the period on screen (upcoming and live ones stay).
- **Calls from Chat no longer fill the calendar** — they're on the new Calls page. Only scheduled meetings and "Meet now" meetings show here.
- **"Live" is now real for "Meet now"**: such a meeting counts as live only while someone is in it; when everyone has left it shows as ended (before, every call looked "live" for an hour, hence "13 live now").
- **Status box** (right side) now says which period it counts (Today / This week / This month) and lists Live (going on now), Upcoming (not started yet) and Ended (over) with their numbers.
- No SQL. Files: `MeetingsController.cs`, `Views/Meetings/Index.cshtml`, `shankhnaad.css`.

## Clients page header: fixed vanishing buttons, new animation
- **Bug fixed**: after switching tabs the header buttons could disappear (even Refresh and Print) until a refresh. Cause: the buttons' page-load "pop-in" starts invisible, and stopping it left them invisible. They're now pinned visible before any animation.
- **New animation**: opening **Plans and limits**, New client, Edit, Delete, Export and Pause / Resume are **gathered into the Refresh button** one after another — each glides toward it, shrinks and softly blurs away — then Print slides over to close the gap and Refresh gives a small pulse. Going back to **Clients**, they **fan out of Refresh** to their places one by one with a gentle settle. Only movement, fading and blur are animated, so it stays smooth.

## Add people to a call that's already going
- In a call, the side panel has **Add people** (under the list of who's in the call). It opens a list of your team with photos and status: **Available**, **On another call**, **Offline · can't ring**, **In this call**. Search by name, tick one or more, press **Ring N**.
- Their screen shows the ringing card with the ringtone, as for any call; **Accept** takes them straight into this call. People already in the call see "Sandy is adding Priya".
- You get a note "Ringing Priya…", and who couldn't be rung because they're offline.
- A one-to-one call that someone was added to becomes a small group call: when one person leaves, the others stay (instead of the call ending for everyone).
- Only people in the call can add people; guests can't. No SQL.
- Files: `ChatDtos.cs`, `ChatHub.cs`, `Views/Meetings/Room.cshtml`, `room.js`, `site.css`.
